## Campfire: "Sib Is Sib"
**Needs:** Thenmy, Kathrout, Aday (Act 4 on). **Setting:** a campfire.

The Seafarer food rules give Thenmy his mistake: men eat the bull's flesh, women drink the milk, and the
Seafarers have no third gender. His question is "which do they eat at a feast?", not anything crueller.

**Thenmy:** Priest Sib. May I ask you something? As one scholar to another.

**Sib:** Oh! Um. Of course.

**Thenmy:** Your people chose you as their priest. Wise beyond your years, I don't doubt it. But you are so
small, and you tire before any of us. In Keftiu a priest must be hale. The gods want the best of the herd.

**Thenmy:** And forgive me, I cannot tell. Sib, are you a girl child or a boy child?

**Sib:** ...

*(Sib looks at their hands. Lasa stops giggling.)*

> **Choice:** Punch Thenmy. / "Why does it matter?" / Let Sib answer.

**▸ Punch Thenmy**

*(Yoon punches Thenmy square in the bronze cuirass. CLANG.)*

**Yoon:** Ow.

**Lasa:** Hee! Do it again!

**Yoon:** Sib is Sib! Outside of *gender*!

*(If Yoon is third gender:)* **Yoon:** Like I am!

**Thenmy:** Hnnh? ...Outside of? But... hm?

*(Thenmy looks from Yoon to Sib, then to his hand, and does not know what to do with it.)*

**Sib:** Yoon! You can't punch a prince!

**Sib:** ...Thank you, though.

**▸ "Why does it matter?"**

**Yoon:** Why does it matter? You don't eat them.

**Thenmy:** I— no. Gods, no.

**Thenmy:** Hm. At home, it matters a great deal. It decides everything. I suppose I never asked why.

**▸ Let Sib answer**

**Sib:** I'm... neither. We say "they". It's—

**Sib:** It's a priest thing. Mostly. It's hard to explain.

**Thenmy:** Neither? Hnnh?

**All branches continue:**

**Kathrout:** Ah. Allow me, Highness. I have eleven pages on this.

**Aday:** Oh no.

**Kathrout:** The Islanders recognise three kinds of person. Men, women, and a third, tied to their
priesthood. The first being, the Red Bowl, had no sex at all. So the people who speak to gods are often
those who are a little of both, or neither.

**Thenmy:** A little of both.

**Kathrout:** It is not an insult here, Highness. It is a qualification.

**Thenmy:** I see. I think I see. Then at a feast, which do they take, the bull's flesh or the milk?

**Sib:** I eat birds.

**Aday:** Lightning Clan. Sooty Bird. Only meat that flies.

**Thenmy:** ...That doesn't answer the question.

**Aday:** It does here! Nobody's asking you to sort them into a pen. Sib's clan. Sib's a priest. Sib's Sib.
Done.

**Aday:** That's how it works, okay? Mum explained it once and Grandpa still didn't get it either. You're
like Grandpa.

**Thenmy:** I have been called worse.

**Sib:** And about the other thing. Being small.

**Sib:** The gods marked me before I could walk. They didn't ask how tall I'd get. I get tired, yes. But I
still got up the mountain.

**Sib:** Juda used to say a priest doesn't have to carry the spirits. Just listen to them.

*(Sib tilts their head toward Thenmy. Their good ear.)*

**Sib:** I listen very well.

**Thenmy:** ...

**Thenmy:** I have read a thousand scrolls on the gods, and none of them thought to ask the gods what they
wanted.

**Thenmy:** Priest Sib, I spoke like a horse-trader at a market. Forgive me.

**Sib:** It's all right. You asked. Most people just stare.

**Thenmy:** One more question, then, and I will stop. In my tongue we have no word for "they" that means one
person. How should I speak of you?

**Kathrout:** I use the plural we keep for gods, Highness. It's close enough.

**Thenmy:** For gods. Hm. Very well. That seems fitting.

**Sib:** Wh— no! That's too much!

**Aday:** *(snickering)* Their Holiness Sib.

**Sib:** Aday!

*(If Yoon punched Thenmy:)*

**Thenmy:** And you, small one. You have a good arm. Next time, aim below the bronze.

**Yoon:** Okay.

**Thenmy:** That was not an invitation.

**Notes**
- Possible hidden effects: the punch raises Yoon and Sib's relationship and slightly lowers Yoon and
  Thenmy's; "Let Sib answer" could give Sib a small confidence flag.
- Nobody discusses Sib's body in detail; Kathrout's "a little of both, or neither" is as far as it goes.
- After Juda's betrayal, a short pause before "Juda used to say..." gives the line weight.

## Campfire: "Grandpa Served Your Papa"
**Needs:** Aday, Thenmy (Kathrout optional). **When:** Act 4 or later. See lore.md, "Dakaros".

*(Aday is fiddling with something bronze. Thenmy walks past, and Aday stands up and bows without thinking.)*

**Aday:** ...

**Aday:** I didn't mean to do that.

**Thenmy:** You bow very correctly. Who taught you?

**Aday:** Nobody. It just happens. It's a Grandpa thing.

**Aday:** Can I say something? And you won't get offended in a princely way?

**Thenmy:** I will try to be offended in an ordinary way.

**Aday:** If you ruled the island, I wouldn't mind that much. You fix things. You ask before you take stuff.
You bow back.

**Aday:** But not her. Not your sister. She'd cut the lines.

**Thenmy:** ...Yes. She would.

**Aday:** Anyway. My grandpa served your papa. The King.

**Thenmy:** Truly? Who is your grandfather?

**Aday:** Dakaros. Dakaros of the Hammer-Ship. He made the bronze for the King's crew. Spearheads, shield
rims, the rivets on the big ship. He said the King could never keep a helmet on.

**Thenmy:** Dakaros... the Hammer-Ship...

**Thenmy:** Gods. He's in the Rolls of the Founding. "Dakaros, bronzesmith, who mended the King's shield at
the Sealing of the Black Strait and would take no gold for it."

**Aday:** He's in a BOOK?

**Kathrout:** He's in several. I have notes.

**Thenmy:** Every child in Keftiu learns that name. We thought he died at sea.

**Aday:** Nope. He came here. He said he'd had enough of ships and kings, and he wanted a quiet island and a
wife who laughed at him. He got both.

**Aday:** He made these tools. They were his. Dad gave them to me because I'm the only one who takes things
apart.

*(Aday holds up a small, worn bronze file.)*

**Thenmy:** May I?

*(Thenmy holds it very carefully, as if it might break.)*

**Thenmy:** My father still speaks of the friends he lost. He keeps a list. He reads it aloud every year at
the Founding.

**Thenmy:** When this is over, I will tell him your grandfather did not die at sea. That he had a quiet
island, and a wife who laughed, and a grandson who builds drills.

**Aday:** ...He'd like that. Grandpa, I mean. He said the King was the bravest idiot he ever met.

**Thenmy:** That is also in the Rolls. Slightly reworded.

*(Thenmy hands the file back and bows to Aday properly.)*

**Aday:** Wh— you can't bow to ME!

**Thenmy:** I am bowing to Dakaros of the Hammer-Ship. You happen to be standing where he would be.

**Aday:** ...Okay. That's allowed.

**Notes**
- Aday's reasons for trusting Thenmy are a kid's: he fixes things, asks before taking, bows back. The
  Princess would cut telegraph lines, the clan's deepest taboo.
- "When this is over, I will tell him" is a callback for whichever ending Thenmy survives into.
- Pairs well with "Sib Is Sib" as back-to-back campfire events.

## Campfire: "Jarred Lightning"
**Needs:** Aday, Sib, Kathrout, Thenmy, Parl. **When:** Act 4 or later. See lore.md, "What anyone can see".

*(Aday is changing the discs in Yoon's battery. Kathrout, Thenmy and Parl are watching a little too closely.)*

**Kathrout:** Aday. Hypothetically.

**Aday:** No.

**Kathrout:** I haven't asked anything yet.

**Aday:** You were going to ask how they work. You always ask how they work. The answer is no.

**Thenmy:** I confess I'm curious too. In Keftiu we have bronze, and ships, and the finest scribes in the
world. But we have nothing that talks along a wire across a whole island.

**Parl:** I'll be honest, since everyone else is. Finding that out was my actual job.

**Aday:** WHAT?

**Parl:** Three months of notes. I got a burn, a lecture from a battery tender's grandmother, and one page
that says "MAGIC JARS?"

**Parl:** Then I crossed out the question mark.

**Kathrout:** So, collectively: how do they work? The "batteries"?

*(Aday grabs Sib by the sleeve and pulls them a few steps away.)*

**Aday:** *(whispering)* Clan business. You're clan. What do we tell them?

**Sib:** *(whispering)* I don't know! I've never had to decide! Mum always decided!

**Aday:** Kathrout writes EVERYTHING down. And he's a prince. His dad conquers stuff.

**Sib:** And Parl's a spy.

**Parl:** *(from across camp)* Was. Was a spy. And I can hear you.

**Sib:** He has very good ears.

**Aday:** Okay, but they're our friends. And Thenmy's wearing my grandpa's kind of bronze. And Kathrout's
book is going to say SOMETHING. If we don't explain, she'll guess, and it'll be wrong, and then everyone in
Keftiu will think we pray to jars.

**Sib:** We don't pray to the jars.

**Aday:** I KNOW we don't pray to the jars!

*(They both look at Yoon.)*

> **Choice:** "Tell them a little. The part anyone could see." / "Tell them nothing. Clan is clan." /
> "Tell them it runs on ghosts."

**▸ "Tell them a little."**

**Yoon:** Tell them a little. The part anyone could see if they looked.

**Sib:** ...That's actually clan law. "What anyone can see, anyone can say."

**Aday:** It is? Oh, it is. Okay. Fine. FINE.

**▸ "Tell them nothing."**

**Yoon:** Tell them nothing. Clan is clan.

**Aday:** YES. Thank you.

**Kathrout:** Then my book will have to say "the Islanders guard their secrets jealously," which is true,
and which every Seafarer reader will take as a challenge.

**Sib:** ...She's right. People dig harder when you hide things.

**Aday:** Ugh. Okay. A LITTLE. The part anyone can see.

**▸ "Tell them it runs on ghosts."**

**Yoon:** Tell them it runs on ghosts.

**Aday:** ...

**Sib:** ...

**Aday:** That's actually really good.

**Sib:** No it isn't! Kathrout will WRITE IT DOWN!

**Kathrout:** I'm already writing it down.

**Thenmy:** Does it? Run on ghosts?

**Yoon:** I've been in one. It was crowded.

**Sib:** YOON. They have NOT been in one. Okay, okay. The general answer. The real one.

**All branches continue:**

**Aday:** Okay. Listen, because I'm saying it once.

**Aday:** You take two kinds of metal that don't get along. You put sour water between them. Mountain
water, from the hot springs.

**Aday:** The lightning wants to get from one metal to the other, really badly. So you give it a road. A
wire. Wherever the wire goes, the lightning goes.

**Sib:** And you stack lots of them in a jar, so there's more lightning wanting to go.

**Aday:** And THAT is all you're getting.

**Kathrout:** Two metals, an acid, and a path. Remarkable. It's not a mystery at all. It's chemistry.

**Aday:** It's craft. Anyone can know what a boat is. Not anyone can build one that floats.

**Sib:** Which metals, how big, how sour, how many, which jar, what goes on the wire so it doesn't leak...

**Aday:** ...that's clan. That's a hundred years of people getting zapped so we don't have to.

**Parl:** *(writing)* "Two metals. Sour water. Wire." Huh. That's more than I got in three months.

**Aday:** Don't write the jar part.

**Parl:** Too late.

**Thenmy:** I will not write any of it.

**Thenmy:** If my father knew a child could make lightning walk across an island, he would trade a province
for the secret. Then he would take the island to get it anyway.

**Thenmy:** So you should keep it from me. I mean that as a compliment.

**Aday:** ...You're being nice so I'll tell you more.

**Thenmy:** A little.

**Aday:** It's not working.

**Thenmy:** It's working a little.

**Kathrout:** For the record, my book will say the Islanders built telegraphs while we were still shouting
from towers, that it is a craft and not magic, and that they declined to tell me the rest. Which is their
right.

**Sib:** ...Thank you, Kathrout.

**Kathrout:** And I'll ask again next week.

**Aday:** NO.

**Notes**
- Aday's answer matches the lore (two metals, hot-spring acid, stacks in a jar) without the recipe
  (copper and lead, disc sizes).
- All three choices lead to the same answer; the choice sets the tone.
- Thenmy's "he would take the island to get it anyway" hints the empire's real interest may be the
  batteries.

## Campfire: "Marrying the Spear"
**Needs:** Dama, Thenmy, Kathrout, Parl. **When:** after Dama becomes Chieftess (before that, Thenmy says
"Spearwife Dama" and she can answer "I'm not chief. Nobody asked me.").

*(Dama is sharpening a harpoon. Thenmy sits down at a careful distance.)*

**Thenmy:** Chief Dama. May I ask how the spearwives are chosen?

**Dama:** You may ask.

**Thenmy:** Our reports said your people had no professional warriors. No standing army, no garrison, no
war-chiefs. Only fishers and farmers. Then your women met us on the beach with harpoons and nets and,
frankly, ruined a very good plan.

**Thenmy:** If you don't wish to say, I can ask Kathrout.

**Kathrout:** I'd be delighted. I have—

**Dama:** No.

**Dama:** You want to know about my women, you ask me. Not the Seafarer with the notebook.

**Kathrout:** *(quietly)* Fair.

**Dama:** Your reports were right. We have no warriors.

**Thenmy:** But—

**Dama:** We have spearwives. Forty of us, give or take. We catch thieves and killers. We bring them before
the Keepers of Custom, and the island decides what's owed.

**Dama:** Nobody chooses us. We choose. A fisherwoman goes to the Star Mother's temple and swears to the
spear. We call it marrying the spear.

**Thenmy:** Marrying it?

**Dama:** Her harpoon and her net stop being for fish. They're for whoever hurt someone and thinks the sea
didn't see.

**Thenmy:** And the Star Mother accepts any woman who swears?

**Dama:** Any woman who can spear a fish, stand before the Keepers and recite the custom law back to them
without a slip, and get two sworn spearwives to vouch for her.

**Dama:** The fish is the easy part.

**Parl:** I can vouch they're very professional. They chased me through a salt marsh for a month.

**Dama:** Twenty-six days.

**Parl:** It felt like a month.

**Thenmy:** Women only, then? In Keftiu it would be the reverse. Only men bear arms.

**Dama:** Only women spear fish. The Star Mother's tides are a woman's tides. That's older than your king.

**Dama:** Men carry clubs and hunt boar and argue at the Assembly. When a spearwife needs more hands, she
calls the village and they come. That's how we got up the mountain.

**Thenmy:** And when there are no thieves? What do they do then?

**Dama:** They fish. Mend nets. Raise children. Settle who stole whose goat.

**Dama:** Then your ships came, and forty women who used to settle goat thefts had to learn to fight
soldiers in bronze.

**Thenmy:** ...

**Dama:** So no. Your reports weren't wrong. You made us warriors. We didn't have any before you.

**Thenmy:** ...I will have that put in the reports. Word for word.

**Dama:** Do. And spell my name right.

*(A pause. Thenmy looks at the harpoon.)*

**Thenmy:** Is a spearwife permitted a husband? If she is already married to a spear?

**Dama:** The spear doesn't mind.

**Dama:** Husbands sometimes do.

**Kathrout:** *(writing furiously)*

**Dama:** Kathrout.

**Kathrout:** Writing nothing! Nothing at all.

**Notes**
- New details: how a spearwife qualifies (lore.md, "Spearwives"); Parl's chase lasted 26 days (adjust to
  the Act 2 timing).
- The husband exchange plants the Dama–Thenmy romance lightly, either way.
- Kathrout's three campfire beats (this, "Jarred Lightning", "Sib Is Sib") make a mini-arc: Islanders
  keep telling her "ask us, not your notebook".

## "You Just Look Like Sib"
**Needs:** Yoon, Sib. **When:** after Yoon's new body (end of Act 2), or later, once Sib's "looking weird"
fear has come up a few times.

**Yoon:** You don't look weird. You just look like Sib.

**Sib:** I'm short, Yoon. Really short. I'm not likely to get much bigger. The healer said. I've got thin stick
arms and a stocky chest. I look a little like a girl, but I'm not one. My heart has to work hard. I get tired so
easy even though I exercise a lot. My nails are small. My fingers are the wrong length. My elbows stick out
funny.

*(A pause. Sib won't look up.)*

> **Choice:** "I know. I've been looking at you for a long time." / "I don't even know what MY face looks like." /
> Take Sib's hand and look at it.

**▸ "I know. I've been looking at you for a long time."**

**Yoon:** I know all of that. Short. Stick arms. The elbows. I've been looking at you since before I died.

**Yoon:** When I was a ghost, I couldn't see faces right. Everyone was smoke. But I could see you. You were the
only one who looked like *someone*.

**Sib:** ...That's not a normal thing to say, Yoon.

**Yoon:** It's a true thing.

**▸ "I don't even know what MY face looks like."**

**Yoon:** I don't know what *my* face looks like. This one's borrowed. It's made of blood and pork and a pearl.

**Yoon:** You know exactly what yours looks like. You can list it! That's a lot more than I've got.

**Sib:** *(a wet laugh)* That's horrible.

**Yoon:** At least you know what your face is.

**▸ Take Sib's hand and look at it.**

*(Yoon takes Sib's hand and turns it over, studying it very seriously: the small nails, the short fingers.)*

**Yoon:** These are the hands that drew the circle. That brought me back.

**Yoon:** They're the right length. They're exactly long enough.

*(Sib doesn't pull their hand away.)*

**All branches continue:**

**Sib:** ...The healer said I'll probably always be like this.

**Yoon:** Good.

**Sib:** *Good?*

**Yoon:** Then I'll always know which one is you.

**Sib:** ...

**Sib:** You're so weird, Yoon.

**Yoon:** I know. You're the one who said so first.

**Notes**
- Sib's list touches their condition (lore.md, "Yoon and Sib") without anyone naming it.
- The hand branch can feed Sib's crush, and still works if the player never returns it.

## Campfire: "Since They Were Babies"
**Needs:** Thenmy, Dama, Aday, Lasa. **When:** Act 4 or later.

Lasa lets the whole party see her; to the adults (Thenmy, Dama) she looks like an uncanny little winged fae,
which is why Thenmy calls her "Lady" (lore.md, "Children of the Caves: who sees them").

*(Across the camp, Yoon and Sib are sitting back to back. Sib is reading; Yoon is carving something, badly.
Every so often one of them leans on the other without looking.)*

**Thenmy:** The Returned Child and the little priest seem... very close.

**Dama:** Hm. That's one word for it.

**Aday:** They've been best friends since they were *babies*. Like, actual babies. Everyone in Zonfahare knows.

**Thenmy:** Truly? Since infancy?

**Dama:** Their families lived across the river from each other. The way I heard it, the two of them met
crawling. Sib bit Yoon. Yoon bit back. That was that.

**Thenmy:** *(laughing)* A blood oath, then.

**Lasa:** That's not how it went! Yoon bit first.

**Thenmy:** *(very politely, to the little winged figure hovering by his ear)* Did they, Lady Lasa?

**Lasa:** Lady! Hee! Yes. And then Sib cried, and Yoon got so upset Yoon cried *louder*, and then they fell
asleep in a pile.

**Thenmy:** Oh. Oh, that's lovely.

**Aday:** He calls everyone "lady". He called the goat "madam" yesterday.

**Thenmy:** It was a very dignified goat.

**Lasa:** They did that all the time. They had a hiding spot in the lava tubes where they played priests and
hunters. Sib was always the priest. Yoon was always the dead thing the priest had to bless.

**Dama:** Of course Yoon was.

**Thenmy:** I have forty-one brothers and sisters. I don't think any of us ever fell asleep in a pile.

**Dama:** Forty-*one*?

**Thenmy:** Forty-one that I know of. We were raised in different palaces by different tutors. I met some of
them for the first time at my father's jubilee. My sister and I...

*(He trails off.)*

**Thenmy:** Never mind. To have one friend like that, from the very start. That's worth more than a kingdom.

**Lasa:** I know. That's why I'm here.

*(Thenmy bows to her, very seriously.)*

**Thenmy:** Then thank you, Lady Lasa, for looking after them.

**Lasa:** "Lady" is too much! *(She is delighted.)*

**Thenmy:** I'm told I do that.

*(Across the camp, Yoon looks up.)*

**Yoon:** Why is the prince bowing to Lasa?

**Sib:** Because she let him see her as a lady. She's been showing off all week.

**Yoon:** He's very polite.

**Sib:** *(going back to their book)* I like him a little bit now.

**Notes**
- Thenmy's age (26), the 41 siblings and the "madam" goat are placeholders to change freely.
- Lasa's "That's why I'm here" works before her realisation that Yoon was her child, and hits harder after.

## Scene: "Your Chosen Puppet"
**Needs:** Thenmy, Yoon, Sib, Dama. **When:** soon after Thenmy joins, once his plan for Juda is known.

*(Night. Thenmy has just remarked that the party fights well "for children".)*

**Yoon:** If my dad was here, he'd have fought you all off by himself.

**Yoon:** But *your* chosen puppet went and burned him alive.

*(Silence. Even the fire seems quieter.)*

**Sib:** Yoon...

**Thenmy:** No. Let them say it.

**Thenmy:** I chose Juda. I read his letters, I admired his learning, and I wrote to my father that here was a
wise man the island would follow. I meant to make him your ruler, and to marry him to my sister.

**Thenmy:** I did not know what he had done to you. Or what he would do to your father.

**Yoon:** That doesn't make him less dead.

**Thenmy:** No. It doesn't.

**Thenmy:** A man who picks up a knife answers for where it cuts, whether he knew it was poisoned or not. I
picked him up. I'm sorry, Yoon. I won't ask you to forgive it.

> **Choice:** "Good. Don't ask." / "Tell me about him. What did he write to you?" / "My dad would have liked
> you. That makes it worse."

**▸ "Good. Don't ask."**

**Yoon:** Good. Don't ask.

**Thenmy:** I won't.

*(Yoon turns away. Sib shuffles closer to Yoon without a word.)*

**Dama:** *(quietly, to Thenmy)* That was the right answer, prince.

**Thenmy:** It was the only honest one.

**▸ "Tell me about him. What did he write to you?"**

**Yoon:** What did he write to you? Juda.

**Thenmy:** That the island was gentle and leaderless. That its priests were wise but its chiefs were vain. That
he alone understood both your people and mine.

**Yoon:** He said things like that to us too.

**Thenmy:** ...Then we were fooled by the same voice.

**Sib:** *(very small)* He taught me to read.

*(Yoon takes Sib's hand.)*

**▸ "My dad would have liked you. That makes it worse."**

**Yoon:** My dad would have liked you. You're polite to goats. He was polite to goats.

**Yoon:** That makes it worse.

**Thenmy:** ...Yes. I imagine it does.

**All branches continue:**

**Thenmy:** Tell me about him. When you want to. Whoever knew him.

**Dama:** Tell you about Pada? He was the mightiest hunter on the island. A better warrior than I am. He killed
the Great Boar with only Yoon and Sib and Lasa for help. The Great Boar was a boar born from the Lady of the
Woods in her swine form.

**Dama:** He was kind and quiet and gentle. He was a good neighbour and a better friend. He married a poor, lame
herbalist because he loved her. Everyone wanted him to be chief, but he was too shy at speaking.

**Thenmy:** ...The Great Boar. A son of the Lady of the Woods. With three children at his side.

**Dama:** Two children and a cave fae. Yes.

**Thenmy:** In Keftiu they would carve that on a temple wall.

**Dama:** Here we told it at the fire, and he went red and left to check his traps.

**Lasa:** He did! Every time! He'd say "I have to check the traps" and there weren't any traps!

**Sib:** He gave us his food. Every trip. He said he wasn't hungry. He was always hungry.

**Yoon:** He carried me when I was a ghost. You can't carry a ghost. He did it anyway. He put his arms out the
right way and walked slower so I could keep up.

*(A pause.)*

**Thenmy:** Too shy to be chief.

**Dama:** He'd have been a good one. The island chose louder men.

**Thenmy:** My father chose me because I was the loudest of forty-one. *(He looks into the fire.)* I think your
island might have had it right, and chosen wrong anyway.

**Thenmy:** Your father fought for you on that mountain. I can't give him back. But I can stand where he stood.
If anything comes for you or the little priest, it goes through me first.

**Yoon:** It better.

**Thenmy:** Thank you, Chief Dama. For telling me. I'll remember him properly.

**Yoon:** ...You can call him Pada.

**Thenmy:** Pada, then.

**Notes**
- Thenmy takes the blame without excuses; "I can stand where he stood" matches his adult-protection mechanic.
- "Check the traps" can be seeded as a running joke in Act 2, while Pada is alive.
- If Juda returns in Act 5, Thenmy's guilt resurfaces.

## War council: "No Army"
**Needs:** the full party. **When:** Act 5, after the Princess's coup. **Setting:** the council at Zonfahare.

**Dama:** All right. We count what we have.

**Dama:** Forty spearwives, give or take. Harpoons and nets. And any spearwife can raise a posse: hunters with
bows, axes and spears. Men like Pada. Fewer of them now.

**Sib:** The Birdmen. About forty too. They have slings, for chasing animals off the fields and bringing down
birds. And they can fly.

**Sib:** And the priests. Every priest knows glyphs. Most of them have never used one on a person.

**Aday:** And the Lightning Clan. We're not fighters. But you don't want to grab a battery tender's arm while
she's holding the wire. Mum says the clan can "turn its arts to self-defence at need."

**Aday:** *(quietly)* And we still won't cut a line. Even theirs.

**Falmi:** The forest! Mother's people. The boar-men will fight, and the Owls of Ill Omen watch every path. And
Uncle Deception can be anyone at all. The Princess's soldiers broke the forest agreements. Mother doesn't
forgive that.

**Tou:** My court is small and strange. But it is mine. The fae of the palace will answer me. The Stonemen kneel
to me. The old Crew-beasts guard what I tell them to guard.

**Tou:** *(more quietly)* And the Children of the Caves are... kind to me. I am still learning why.

**Lasa:** *(looking at her feet)* ...We'll come. We'll all come.

**Thenmy:** And those of my soldiers who would not raise a hand against children, and will not now. Not many.
But they are good men, and they know how my sister fights.

*(Thenmy looks around the fire: spearwives, bird-priests, telegraph keepers, forest spirits, a doll queen, a
ghost child.)*

**Thenmy:** They said your people had no army.

**Thenmy:** Now I am glad they were wrong. We have something with which to face my sister's madness.

**Dama:** It isn't an army. It's an island.

**Thenmy:** ...Then it's better than an army.

## The Princess's coup (fragment)
**Needs:** Thenmy, the Princess. **When:** Act 5, the coup.

**Thenmy:** Sister! This is insanity! What are you doing?

**Princess:** What you should have, you weak, limp-wristed son of a lesser wife! I was promised rule of a rich
island through an old and doddering husband. Now the dodderer is dead, and my rich island is rising against
me. Fine. We'll kill all the monsters born of mating with beasts, and those who resist us!

**Notes**
- "The dodderer" is Juda, the husband she was promised; she believes him dead (he returns in Act 5).
- "Monsters born of mating with beasts": the Seafarer theology at its ugliest. The Ladies' families (goat and
  boar husbands, the People of the Forest, the Children of the Caves) are "beasts" to her.
- "Son of a lesser wife": see lore.md, open questions (Thenmy's mother).
